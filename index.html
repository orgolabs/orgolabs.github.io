Diastereoselective reduction

Reduction of Camphor

Sodium borohydride turns a rigid bicyclic ketone into isoborneol and borneol — the textbook case of steric approach control. Rotate all three, watch the gem-dimethyl bridge do the work.

C₁₀H₁₆O  — NaBH₄ →  isoborneol + borneol  (C₁₀H₁₈O)

Esterification · multistep

Synthesis of Aspirin

Acetylate the phenol of salicylic acid with acetic anhydride — the two-step route from oil of wintergreen, proven by the FTIR fingerprint. Based on the Olmsted (1998) experiment.

C₇H₆O₃ + (CH₃CO)₂O  — H₃PO₄ →  aspirin  (C₉H₈O₄)

How these are built

no hand-waving

Real geometry

Structures are the actual 3D conformers from PubChem, rendered with 3Dmol.js — not sketches. Drag, zoom, highlight the atoms that change.

Fact-checked

Every claim is cross-checked against the primary literature; references carry resolvable DOIs and honest open-vs-subscription labels.

The real procedure

The actual lab handout, step by step, with the mechanism and a live, limiting-reagent-aware yield calculator.

Open & reusable

MIT-licensed, structures public-domain. New labs start from a public template.
